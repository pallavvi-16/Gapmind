# 🧠 GapMind

> A skill-gap analysis platform designed to help students identify their strengths, discover areas for improvement, and prepare for career opportunities.

🚧 **Project Status: Work in Progress**

GapMind is currently under active development. The core project structure and initial features have been implemented, and additional functionality will be added in future updates.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to understand whether their current skills match the requirements of their desired career or placement opportunities.

GapMind aims to provide a simple platform where users can assess their skills, identify gaps, and understand which areas they need to improve.

---

## 💡 Project Objective

The main objective of GapMind is to:

- Identify users' current skills and knowledge.
- Analyze potential skill gaps.
- Help users understand areas that need improvement.
- Provide a structured platform for assessment and career preparation.
- Help students become more placement-ready.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👤 Onboarding
Provides an initial interface for users to enter and understand their profile and assessment journey.

### 📝 Skill Assessment
Allows users to take assessments to evaluate their current knowledge and skills.

### 📊 Results
Displays assessment results and helps users understand their performance.

### 📈 Dashboard
Provides a central place to view relevant user information and progress.

### 💼 Placement Preparation
Includes a dedicated section focused on placement-related preparation.

### 🎤 Interview Preparation
Provides an interface for interview preparation and practice.
